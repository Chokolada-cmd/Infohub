About the Institute

Who We Are

A place where every student is believed in, guided, and equipped to excel — in results, and in mindset.

Founder & Director

Mr Ntsane Masiloane

Institute of Mathematics

Mr Masiloane founded the Institute of Mathematics on a simple conviction: every learner carries the ability to excel — what they need is not more pressure, but the right perspective, the right habits, and someone who believes in them.

His approach combines rigorous academic teaching in Maths, Physics and Accounting with what he calls the paradigm shift — a change in how a student sees themselves, their ability, and their future. Rather than chasing marks alone, learners are taught to build vision, discipline and imagination as the foundation of lasting achievement.

Under his leadership, the Institute has grown into a 6-week programme that trains not only subject knowledge, but the higher mental faculties that separate top achievers from the rest.

"Imagine yourself a top achiever."

Our Mission

Guiding Every Student to Excel

To inspire and empower students to achieve excellence in their academic pursuits through innovative teaching methods and a supportive learning environment.

We are dedicated to providing high-quality education in Mathematics, Science and Accounting. Our experienced faculty and comprehensive curriculum ensure that students receive the best possible learning experience — one built on understanding, not memorisation.

Belief in Every Learner

02

Understanding Over Memory

03

Habits Build Excellence

Excellence is not a once-off event — it is a habit built daily through order, movement and consistent effort.
