Every plan

What the monthly fee covers.

This is the part most providers skip. It is the reason a Sable engagement is a service, not a handover.

Account management

New users, departures, permission changes, group restructures. Handled quietly, usually same day.

Backup monitoring

We watch your cloud backups, verify restore points, and flag anything that stops working. A backup you've never tested is not a backup.

Security checks

Two-factor status, password hygiene, sharing exposure, sign-in anomalies. A short monthly report you can actually read.

User support

WhatsApp for quick questions, remote sessions for real problems. No ticket system, no call centre, no queue.
