The Shift

From a burial society
to a living society.

For generations, stokvels have carried us. We pooled to bury each other with dignity — and that mattered. But a people who only save for the end never get to see the beginning. Sisonke turns the same trusted circle towards a different horizon.

The Old Stokvel

"We prepare to bury each other well."

  • Contributions released only at a funeral
  • A lifetime of paying in, one payout out
  • Families meet in grief, not in joy
  • Memories never made — only memorials
The Life Experience Stokvel

"We prepare to live well together."

  • Contributions released as trips, twice a year
  • Every member travels — no one is left behind
  • Families meet in joy, on a mountain, at the sea
  • An inheritance of photographs and stories

"A stokvel is not a bank account. It is a promise between people. We simply changed what the promise is for."

— The Sisonke Manifesto

How the circle works

Four steps to a full life.

  1. 01

    Gather your circle

    Five people or fifty. Family, colleagues, church group, or join an open circle and meet your travel family.

  2. 02

    Choose your contribution

    From R500 to R5 000 a month per person. Everyone sees the same transparent ledger — no hidden fees, ever.

  3. 03

    Vote on the destination

    Every member gets a vote. Two departures a year — one local weekend, one flagship trip.

  4. 04

    Go. And go again.

    We handle flights, stays, guides and logistics. You handle the photographs and the inside jokes.
