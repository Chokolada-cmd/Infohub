About Sable

Narrow by design.

Sable is a South African managed service provider focused exclusively on software and cloud. We do not sell hardware, we do not run cables, and we do not pretend to be a general IT department. That focus is deliberate — it keeps our costs low, lets us work remotely, and means every client gets a partner who genuinely knows the tools they run on.

We work with three markets: schools, tutors and small offices. Each has a different rhythm — the school year, the tutor's calendar, the small office's quiet growth — and each gets a service built around it, not a generic package.

Everything we do is built to be reusable. The booking page we build for one tutor becomes better for the next. The onboarding automation we write for one school gets sharper with every intake. That leverage is how a small team serves many clients without dropping the standard.

How it works

From first call to monthly rhythm.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Thirty minutes. We map what you use today, what is broken, and what you wish were simpler. You leave with a written summary either way.

  2. 02

    Proposal and setup

    A fixed setup fee and a monthly fee, both written down. Once approved, we do the migration, configuration and security work — usually within two weeks.

  3. 03

    Handover and training

    We walk your team through the new setup in a single session, leave a one-page guide, and make sure everyone knows how to reach us.

  4. 04

    Monthly service

    Account management, backup monitoring, security checks and support. A short report each month, a review call each quarter.

FAQ

The questions we get most.

Why software and cloud only?

Hardware is a different business — logistics, warranties, on-site visits. By staying on software and cloud, we keep costs low, work remotely, and go deeper on the tools that actually shape how your organisation runs day to day.

Do you work on-site?

Almost everything is done remotely. For initial setup at a school, we may attend one on-site session if it helps, but the ongoing service is remote by design — it is faster for you and cheaper for both of us.

What does the setup fee cover?

Migration, configuration, security hardening, account creation, and training. It is a one-time fee that gets you onto a clean, working setup. The monthly fee starts after handover.

Can we cancel the monthly service?

Yes. There is no long lock-in. We ask for one month's notice so we can hand over cleanly — admin access, documentation, and a final backup verification. You own your accounts and data throughout.

Do you mark up software licences?

No. Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoom and similar are passed through at cost. We make our money on the managed service, not on reselling software. Google Workspace for Education is free for eligible schools and we help you confirm that.

Is our data safe?

We apply least-privilege access, two-factor authentication, and encrypted backups as standard. We are POPIA-conscious and will sign an NDA or data-processing agreement on request.
