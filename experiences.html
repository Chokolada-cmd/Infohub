Circle of the year

How 24 colleagues from Pretoria went to God's Window.

In 2023 a group of admin staff at a Pretoria logistics firm started a R750-a-month circle. Twelve months later they chartered a bus to Mpumalanga for four days. Nobody missed a payment. Nobody was left behind.

This is what "umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu" looks like in 2024 — not a cheque at a funeral, but a photograph of a colleague seeing the edge of the world for the first time.
