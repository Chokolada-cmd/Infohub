    Step three

    Questions, answered plainly.

    Do I have to pay anything?

    No. Every programme listed here is free to apply for. If anyone asks you for a fee to secure a placement, learnership or grant, it is a scam — walk away and report it.

    Why do you link away instead of taking my application?

    Because the official source is the only place your application is safe. We are a directory, not a middleman. Your ID number, name and phone number should only ever go to the provider itself.

    What does "rolling" mean?

    The programme accepts applications on an ongoing basis rather than within a fixed window. "Open" means there is a closing date — you'll see it on the card. "Unknown" means we couldn't confirm the window, so check the source page.

    What is a certified copy, and where do I get one?

    A photocopy stamped and signed by a Commissioner of Oaths. Any police station or post office does it free of charge. Bring the original and the copy. Certifications are usually valid for three months.

    I don't have matric. Is it over for me?

    No. Some programmes mark matric as "preferred", not "required", and platforms like SA Youth are open regardless. Skills programmes and learnerships also accept candidates without matric — check each advert's requirements.

    How is my information handled?

    The eligibility check runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. We record only anonymous counts — an age band, a province and a matched/not-matched flag — never an ID number, name or phone number.
