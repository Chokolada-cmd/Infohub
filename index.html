The Programme

Paradigm Shifting

We provide a 6-week training programme that changes how a student sees, thinks and performs.

What is a paradigm shift? It is a change in the student's perspective.

At the Institute of Mathematics we hold a firm belief that for rapid change in results, there must first be a change in how you see reality. We want to change your perspective on how we learn and input information — not through our eyes and ears alone, but through our intuition and imagination.

Beyond seeing, hearing and studying, there are higher faculties that students must observe and manipulate in order to succeed. The six higher faculties are Reason, Imagination, Intuition, Perception, Memory and Will. The most important faculty on the journey to success is Imagination. With imagination the world was created. Religious books tell us man was created in God's image — and what is this image? It is the imagination of God. We carry the same power and spirit within us.

Imagine yourself a top achiever. Imagine yourself great. See yourself in the mind, picture greatness, be a visionary. We will teach you how to exercise this mental faculty. We plant pictures into your mind: see yourself achieving distinctions in most subjects, picture yourself receiving that award at school for high marks, see yourself as a Top 10 learner in your grade. Imagine yourself smashing each and every test and exam.

We also teach you how to set goals and achieve them. We believe you are a goal creator.

Foundations

The skills needed for study — and the mental attitude

  • Independence

    Work yourself to success and build a powerful self-image with strength and optimism. Be a self-starter, self-motivated and self-disciplined. Always be at a level where you are working on yourself, for yourself, by yourself — but that does not mean being alone and lonely. Build a network of associates. Don't wait, just do it.

  • Associates

    A great man once said, "Show me your friends and I will show you your future." The people you associate with will either make you or break you. Choose to mix with positive-minded people — the ones who break limits, boundaries and the mental prison of poverty.

  • Attitude

    Attitude is everything: spirit, mindset, vibration. Think yourself great and you will become great. Every thought changes your rate of vibration; as you learn to think greater, deeper, higher and more forcefully, your brain cells build new neurons. Be sure your thoughts are good — they determine who and what you are, and ultimately what you will get.

Requirements

What we ask of every learner

  • Pro-Active

    This is the principle you apply once you are fully independent. Good students are pro-active: they push themselves, they don't wait for a teacher to show them the way — they can see the way themselves. The real secret to outstanding results is to excel in everything you do, including chores at home, cleaning, and working on yourself.

  • Self-Leadership

    You can lead a horse to water, but you can't make it drink. This is where you come in. You must put in the effort. You must believe in yourself. You must be disciplined and put yourself in order. Order + Movement = Growth. Have a vision for yourself, define it yourself and push yourself.

  • Sacrifice

    Give up what you want for what you need. There is a very thin line between a want and a need. Think about sleeping less or waking up early — do you need little rest, or comfortable sleep? Read more, download informative books instead of scrolling social media, watch enriching videos.

Daily habits are key.
