Safe, transparent, yours

A stokvel your gogo would approve of.

  1. 01

    Dedicated circle account

    Every circle gets its own FSCA-compliant account. Members see every rand in and out, in real time.

  2. 02

    Two-to-sign release

    No single person can move the money. Every trip payout needs the circle's elected signatories.

    Everyone votes

    Destinations, dates and budgets are decided by the whole circle — one member, one vote.
