Step Two

Topics

Open a topic to see its lessons. Each lesson includes video and practice.

Lesson 1 Vectors and scalars — the difference, and how to represent and add vectors in one dimension. Lesson 2 Motion in one dimension — position, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration explained with graphs. Lesson 3 Equations of motion — using the four kinematic equations to solve real problems. Lesson 4 Energy — gravitational potential energy, kinetic energy and conservation of mechanical energy.
Lesson 1 Transverse pulses — amplitude, superposition and how pulses interact on a string or spring. Lesson 2 Transverse and longitudinal waves — wavelength, frequency, period and wave speed with worked calculations. Lesson 3 Sound waves — pitch, loudness, quality, and how ultrasound works in medicine and industry.
Lesson 1 Magnetism — magnetic fields of permanent magnets, poles, attraction and repulsion, and the Earth's magnetic field. Lesson 2 Electrostatics — two kinds of charge, charge conservation, quantisation and polarisation. Lesson 3 Electric circuits — emf, potential difference, current, resistance and resistors in parallel.
Lesson 1 Atomic structure — protons, neutrons, electrons and how the periodic table is organised. Lesson 2 Chemical bonding — ionic, covalent and metallic bonds explained with South African examples. Lesson 3 Particle model of matter — states of matter, kinetic theory and changes of state.
